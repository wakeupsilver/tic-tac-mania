THE RULEBOOK

The arena has 9 rings, and each ring is a tic-tac-toe board. Win a ring's tic-tac-toe to capture it. Capture 3 rings in a row to become champion.

WHERE YOU PLAY, THEY GO

The cell you pick inside a ring sends your opponent to the matching ring. Play the top-right cell and they must play in the top-right ring. If that ring is already decided or caged, they get a free move anywhere.

If all 9 rings are decided and nobody has 3 in a row, the player who captured the most rings wins.

HYPE & SPECIAL MOVES (Juiced & MAXIMUM)

You gain hype from moves, big hype from captures, more from combos, and sympathy hype when you get hit. Before placing, spend it on special moves (each one at most once per turn). You can stack as many as you can afford:

🪑 STEEL CHAIR30Smash one enemy piece in an uncaptured ring.
🪢 ROPE BREAK25Ignore where you were sent and play in any open ring.
🤝 TAG TEAM45You take two placements this turn.
😈 HEEL TURN55Turn an enemy piece into yours. This can capture a ring on the spot.
💥 ATOMIC LEG DROP80Wipe an entire ring, captured or not, back to empty.
💪 MAXIMUM FLEX100Your next placement captures that ring instantly.

RING EVENTS (MAXIMUM only)

Every few turns the arena goes nuts: 🌋 Earthquake rotates the whole arena 90° · 🧲 Gravity makes pieces fall · ⛓️ Steel Cage locks a ring · 🪑 Chair Shower gives everyone hype · 🎆 Pyro blows up random pieces · 🌑 Blackout hides every piece · 🔁 Switcheroo swaps all pieces in a ring.

TOOLS

Threat Vision: red means that cell sends your opponent somewhere they can capture a ring, pulsing red means they can win the match from there, and orange hands them a free move. Hint asks the AI for your best placement.

KEYS
